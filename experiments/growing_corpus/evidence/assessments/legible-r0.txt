For 3-of-5 it is one, not three. The resulting Phase 1 requirement remains correct through the hitting-set argument above. Whether the pairwise-overlap sentence is a typo, shorthand relying on an unstated restriction, or a proof error is unresolved. [same artifact, physical PDF p. 10]
